◆ arang — discovery, done differently

Three directions that deliberately avoid the Eventbrite/Meetup "search + card grid" pattern. Each uses what's unique to Arang: human-submitted local supply, per-field confidence, verification, interests, proximity — and an AI that does the work instead of making you search.

Direction A

🗣️ Weekend Concierge

No search bar, no grid. You chat your vibe; Arang curates 3 picks and shows why it chose each. Mirrors the WhatsApp intake side on the discovery side.

Disruption: discovery is a conversation that produces a plan — not a catalog you operate.
Direction B

🗓️ Weekend Planner

Your Saturday–Sunday as a timeline with free-slot gaps Arang offers to fill. The unit is a weekend you assemble, not an event.

Disruption: events-as-a-plannable-itinerary; nobody treats the weekend as the object.
Direction C

🗺️ Local Pulse

An ambient map of your town right now — events pulse near you, bigger/brighter = sooner, verified, popular. Spatial and temporal, not a list.

Disruption: "what's alive near me, soon" as a living map — not a queryable database.

All exploratory, P2+ (consumer discovery is out of P1 scope). No backend; clickable front-end only. Open each and click around.
